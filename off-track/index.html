The complete story.

Max Emilian Verstappen. Dutch. Born 30 September 1997. Four-time Formula 1 World Champion with Oracle Red Bull Racing.

1997

Born into racing

Born on 30 September in Hasselt, Belgium, to racers Jos Verstappen and Sophie Kumpen. Max competes under the Dutch flag.

2001–2005

The first kart

He began karting at four and entered his first competitive season in 2005. Karting became his racing education.

2013

World and European karting titles

Max won the CIK-FIA KZ World Championship and both European KZ and KF titles, alongside WSK titles.

2014

Ten Formula 3 wins

His first season in single-seaters brought ten wins and third place in the FIA European Formula 3 Championship.

2015

Formula 1 at seventeen

Max debuted with Scuderia Toro Rosso in Australia. At 17 years and 166 days, he became the youngest Formula 1 driver.

2016

The youngest Grand Prix winner

Promoted to Red Bull Racing for the Spanish Grand Prix, Max won immediately at 18 years and 228 days.

2017–2020

Building the challenge

Nine more Grand Prix wins over four seasons. Third in the championship in 2019 and 2020.

2021

World champion

Ten Grand Prix victories and 395.5 points delivered Max’s first World Drivers’ Championship.

2022

Back-to-back

Fifteen wins and 454 points secured a second world title. Max raced with the champion’s number 1.

2023

A record season

Nineteen victories in 22 races, ten consecutive wins, 21 podiums and 575 points. A third straight world title.

2024

Four in a row

Nine wins and 437 points. A fourth title secured in Las Vegas, with a remarkable wet-weather victory from 17th in São Paulo.

2025

Fighting to the final race

Eight wins, 15 podiums and 421 points. Second in the championship, two points from the title.

2026

A new era, number 3

Oracle Red Bull Racing’s RB22 and a new race number. 72 career Grand Prix wins as of 7 October, with Bahrain in Malaysia the latest.
